El Niño index, last 3 years

The index is +0.5 or more in an El Niño and -0.5 or less in a La Niña. Source: NOAA.

Temperature and rainfall: IMD gridded data averaged over each district, and Open-Meteo for the most recent months. Rainfall is the total for the month, temperatures are monthly averages of the daily highest and lowest temperature. El Niño index (ONI, 3-month average): NOAA CPC. Indian Ocean Dipole (DMI): NOAA PSL. Live temperature: Open-Meteo. District boundaries: Esri India 2024.
